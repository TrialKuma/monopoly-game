Production art reference sheet for a stylized 3D mobile Monopoly city game. This is an actionable architectural model sheet, not a scene illustration. Theme: French provincial town; warm ivory limestone, blue-grey mansard and terracotta roofs, stone corner blocks, generous cornices, wrought-iron balcony silhouettes, muted sage shutters, amber windows. Differentiation: a townhouse home, a corner patisserie with green fabric awning, a 3-storey boutique hotel with mansard dormers, a small corner apartment mansion with a rounded roof pavilion. FOUR ROWS, each row is ONE of these four building designs. THREE COLUMNS in each row: strict ORTHOGRAPHIC FRONT view, strict ORTHOGRAPHIC RIGHT SIDE view, strict ORTHOGRAPHIC REAR view of the EXACT SAME building. Each row must maintain exact dimensions, roof geometry, colors, floors and openings consistently across the three views. Show complete standalone building including modest stone plinth, no floating broken parts. White matte background, clean thin pale grey grid separators, neutral studio illumination without dramatic shading. Discrete labels FRONT / SIDE / BACK atop columns, row labels HOUSE / SHOP / HOTEL / RESIDENCE. Tall high-resolution sheet, all twelve complete buildings legible, generous separation. Quality target: polished hand-painted toy-like chunky 3D game architecture, softly beveled architectural edges, large readable trim layers, true tile roof rhythm with only a few broad tile rows, inset window frames and jambs, raised sills, clear doorway, simple chimney. Approximately 50 percent more architectural detail than a simple casual city builder, no tiny engraved details, no photorealism, no complex ornament, no huge castles. House and shop two floors; hotel and residence three floors, not skyscrapers. Model proportions compact roughly square footprint; each facade width about 6 meters. No vegetation concealing architecture, no people, no UI beyond the small view/row labels. Every side must be designed with real doors/windows/roof returns rather than blank back walls.
