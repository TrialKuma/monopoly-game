Production art reference sheet for a stylized 3D mobile Monopoly city game. Architectural modeling sheet, not a scene. New York 1920s Art Deco neighborhood: warm red brown brick and buff limestone, deep petrol window glass, oxidized copper accents, broad stepped parapets, vertical pilasters, geometric entrance canopies, recessed storefronts. Four distinct designs: two-storey brick townhouse home, two-storey corner deli with an emerald shopfront, a three-storey boutique Art Deco hotel, a four-storey stepped apartment building with restrained gold vertical trims. Chunky toy architecture, no skinny real skyscrapers. FOUR ROWS, each row one of these four designs. THREE COLUMNS: strict ORTHOGRAPHIC FRONT, strict ORTHOGRAPHIC RIGHT SIDE, strict ORTHOGRAPHIC REAR of the EXACT SAME building. Across each row maintain identical proportions, roof, floor count and openings. Labels FRONT / SIDE / BACK atop, row labels HOUSE / SHOP / HOTEL / RESIDENCE. White background, pale thin grid separators. Tall high-resolution sheet, twelve whole standalone buildings with generous separation. Polished chunky hand-painted 3D mobile game style, gently rounded edges, actual inset window surrounds and sills, stone base, broad visible roof tiles or brick coursing, substantial trim layers. Moderate detail: about 50 percent richer than a basic casual city builder, NO photorealism, NO microscopic decorations. Neutral even lighting, front views axis-aligned and no perspective tilt. No ground scenery, no people, no foliage obscuring facades. All sides designed, not blank walls.
