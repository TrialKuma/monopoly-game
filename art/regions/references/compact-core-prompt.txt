Production art reference sheet for a stylized 3D mobile Monopoly city game. Architectural modeling sheet, not a scene. Japanese Kyoto machiya district: warm ivory plaster, dark cedar timber, charcoal blue grey ceramic tiled gabled roofs with restrained curved eaves, timber lattice screens, noren fabric curtains and small lanterns. Four distinct designs: narrow townhouse home, street tea/sweets shop with a sage noren and lattice storefront, three-storey ryokan inn with timber balconies, corner merchant residence with a roof lookout. Traditional compact urban vernacular, no shrines or pagodas for ordinary housing. FOUR ROWS, each row one of these four designs. THREE COLUMNS: strict ORTHOGRAPHIC FRONT, strict ORTHOGRAPHIC RIGHT SIDE, strict ORTHOGRAPHIC REAR of the EXACT SAME building. Across each row maintain identical proportions, roof, floor count and openings. Labels FRONT / SIDE / BACK atop, row labels HOUSE / SHOP / HOTEL / RESIDENCE. White background, pale thin grid separators. Tall high-resolution sheet, twelve whole standalone buildings with generous separation. Polished chunky hand-painted 3D mobile game style, gently rounded edges, actual inset window surrounds and sills, stone base, broad visible roof tiles or brick coursing, substantial trim layers. Moderate detail: about 50 percent richer than a basic casual city builder, NO photorealism, NO microscopic decorations. Neutral even lighting, front views axis-aligned and no perspective tilt. No ground scenery, no people, no foliage obscuring facades. All sides designed, not blank walls.
